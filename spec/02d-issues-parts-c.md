# 2.D Issues raised by later parts (S11b-S19)

*Spec part 2.D · owner cell C-032 · consolidates the `Issues raised` tables at the end of `spec/11b-requester-ux.md`, `11c-admin-tools.md`, `11d-i18n-copy.md`, `12-public-growth.md`, `13-measurement.md`, `15a-api-core.md`, `15b-api-admin.md`, `16-parameters.md`, `17-build-plan.md`, `18-testing.md` and `19-risks.md`, plus the issue ids that `14-launch-gates.md` cites without a table. Ids are kept exactly as the parts wrote them. No part file was edited. Nothing here is legal advice and nothing was verified against a provider's terms or a law.*

## 2.D.1 How this register was built

- One row per row of each part's table, in the order of the parts. Wording is shortened to one line but not weakened; where a part packs several sub-points into one id (for example `ISS-117` (a) to (k)) the row keeps the sub-point letters. [D]
- Every `fd_ref` was checked against the text of the Founding Document draft v0.1 (FD). The `note` column says `FD ok` when the cited text says what the row says it does, `no fd_ref` when the part cites none, and starts with `recheck:` when the row misstates the FD, with the correct location. A misstated row is kept, not dropped. [D]
- The ids `ISS-117`, `ISS-118` and `ISS-119` were each used by two files (`11c-admin-tools.md` and `11d-i18n-copy.md`) with different content. Both rows are kept under the same id and told apart by the `part` column. Renaming is a decision for the register owner and is not done here (see 2.D.6). [D]
- `spec/14-launch-gates.md` has no `Issues raised` table. It cites `ISS-141`, `ISS-143`, `ISS-144` and `ISS-147` in its text. Those four are listed in 2.D.5 with only what the citing lines say, and with no severity, because none is stated. [D]
- `status` is the status the part gave. `15a-api-core.md` has no status column; it names a decider instead, shown as `unstated (decider: ...)`. [D]

Counts: 68 rows from the eleven tables: 0 `blocking`, 32 `major`, 36 `minor`; plus 4 cited-only ids in 2.D.5. [D]
