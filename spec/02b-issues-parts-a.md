## 2.B.1 How to read this register

- Columns: `id` · `severity` · `part` · `fd_ref` · `issue` (one line, as raised) · `resolution applied` (one line, as applied in the part) · `status` · `note`. [D]
- `status` is the part's own status. S7 has no status column, only `Needs owner`; `yes` is carried here as `needs_owner` and `no` as `proposed` [A]. S5b, S6b and S4 use the S0.4 statuses directly.
- `note` is `-` when the fd_ref was checked and the row is faithful. A note starting `recheck:` means the cited FD location does not support (all of) the row. A note starting `overlap:` or `conflict:` points at another register row; the detail is in 2.B.5. [D]
- Where this register and a part resolve the same issue differently, `spec/02-issues.md` 2.9 item 3 applies: the register wins. This file only reports the difference.
- The parts' paragraphs headed "Convention additions" or "Convention gaps" are lists of names, not issue rows. They are not copied here; `ISS-45` and `ISS-51` are the two rows that carry convention gaps as issues, and 2.8 of the register is where the names are folded. [D]

## 2.B.5 Observations

- **Resolutions that contradict the register or each other.** `ISS-61` lets the levy budget absorb a verification overrun, but `ISS-16` item 3 makes the pool a hard budget and bars Reserve subsidy; `AC-ledger-03` and `T-unit-01` in S6a test the levy draw, so one side must change. `ISS-62` keeps lineage along transfers and has reversals land on the requester, against `ISS-14` item 3 and `ISS-17`. `ISS-41` adds a flag-gated check runner, against `ISS-12` item 1. By 2.9 item 3 the register wins in all three, and S4, S6a and S6b have not yet been brought into line.
- **In-flight work after a policy change has two answers.** `ISS-27` pays a `submitted` cell that passed checks and sets it `voided`; `ISS-76`, `AC-policy-19`, `AC-race-28` and `RC-28` give no credit and requeue it with `attempt` unchanged. The terminal state and the payment both differ. `needs_owner` in `ISS-76`; `ISS-27` is `proposed`.
- **The Reserve outflow list.** FD§4.2 allows starter grants only. `ISS-56` and S5b convention item 10 add `appeal_restitution`; `ISS-16` and `ISS-27` item 3 say a Reserve outflow beyond grants is forbidden or owner-only; `ISS-17` item 4 says lots lost in a ban stay lost, while `ISS-56` restitutes them after an overturned ban. One owner decision (S3.2) settles all four.
- **Duplicates across parts.** `ISS-41`/`ISS-12`; `ISS-62`/`ISS-14`; `ISS-61`/`ISS-16`/`ISS-04`; `ISS-63` (b) (c) (d)/`ISS-18`/`ISS-15`; `ISS-71`/`ISS-23`/`ISS-11`; `ISS-72`/`ISS-23` item 5; `ISS-74`/`ISS-27`; `ISS-75` and S5b's JT-19; `ISS-52`/`ISS-64`/`ISS-79` (file names and heading numbers). The duplicates are not wrong, but each pair needs one owner row at assembly.
- **Name clashes to settle before assembly.** `service_kind` values (`chat_service`, `open_weight` in S7 against `hosted_service`, `open_weight_model` in `ISS-24` and 2.8); `FlagAnswer.note` against `value_text`; `info.automation_limits` against the note in `ISS-23`; three approval predicates for a service (`ISS-71`, `ISS-23`, `ISS-11`); "oldest-first" against earliest-expiry-first (`ISS-63` (a) against `ISS-33`, `ISS-08`).
