## 2.C.1 How to read this register

- One row per row of the part tables, with the id kept exactly. Issue and resolution text is condensed to one line and keeps what the part says, including its status. A part's own statement of its resolution is recorded as written; where this register (`spec/02-issues.md`) records a different resolution, `2.9` of that file says this register wins, and the Observations below name the cases. [D]
- The `note` column holds `recheck:` where the row misstates the FD (with the correct location), `dup:` where another register entry covers the same finding, and a dash otherwise. No row was dropped or weakened.
- Columns: id, severity, part (S-number and file), fd_ref as written by the part, issue, resolution applied, status, note. Statuses are the parts' own (`proposed`, `applied`, `needs_owner`); none is `accepted` or `rejected` yet.
- The `Convention additions` lists at the end of these parts are not issue tables; the names they list are carried by `ISS-83`, `ISS-84` and `ISS-112`, which are the parts' own rows for them.

Counts: 23 issues in all: 0 `blocking`, 14 `major`, 9 `minor`. Status: 15 `proposed`, 2 `applied`, 6 `needs_owner`. `ISS-81` and `ISS-91` describe the register's blocking `ISS-12` and are rated `major` by their parts. [D]

## Observations

- **Code execution, three entries for one finding.** `ISS-81`, `ISS-91` and the register's `ISS-12` are the same finding. The parts rate it `major`, the register `blocking`. `ISS-12` says the platform never executes submitted content in Phases 1 and 2 and specifies no sandbox; `ISS-81` and `ISS-91` allow an isolated runner behind `ff.template.code_change_sandbox`, and 8.15 of 08a still lists `test_run` as the strongest check of `code_change_tests`. Flag-off default is compatible; the runner text is not. Under `2.9` the register wins. needs_owner.
- **What a rejected donor sees.** `ISS-86` (a) shows the `reject.gold_mismatch` reason and accepts that the donor learns the cell was gold; `ISS-111` hides it, together with the audit, duplicate and timing reasons, behind `copy.reject.generic_check`. Contradictory; one rule is needed in S9 and S11.
- **Who pays for verification.** `ISS-85` (c) lets the levy share fund floor-band shortfalls; `ISS-16` of the register says the pool is a hard budget, the Reserve never subsidizes verification and the job stops with `verification_budget_exhausted`. `ISS-85` (b) says no template is `high` in Phase 1, while 8.15 lists `code_change_tests_rs` as `high` and `ISS-16` keeps blanket redundancy for high-risk templates. The register wins; 8.32 needs correcting by its owner.
- **`C9` is claimed three times**: `ISS-15` (mint cap against redeemable capacity), `ISS-84` (8.31 verification warning) and 10a 10.18.3 (`session.member_abs_ttl_s`). Part 9 avoided the clash with `S9-C1`..`S9-C3`. S16.* must renumber; this register does not rename.
- **`security.two_person_actions` has three different changed defaults**: `ISS-22` adds `commons.job_approve`, `ISS-102` adds six actions, 10b adds four (`member.ban`, `disclosure.release`, `report.legal_hold_clear`, `secret.emergency_access`) and overlaps 10a on two. The final default is the union and must be set once in S16.*.
- **Identity names disagree between 10a and 11a.** `ISS-102` says e-mail is never a recovery factor, while the 11a settings wireframe reads "used only for recovery help? S10.* decides". 11a names `createDeviceLinkCode`, `redeemDeviceLinkCode`, `rotateMemberKey`, `identity.device_link_code_ttl_s` and `identity.key_rotation_grace_s`; the tail of 10a names `createDeviceLinkOffer`, `createDeviceLinkClaim`, `createMemberKey` and other parameters. `ISS-112` leaves reconciliation to S15.* and C-022 without saying which side wins. Based on the sections read, not on a full-file comparison.
- **FD citations that needed rechecking**: `ISS-82` (FD§0.2 gives no reviewer count), `ISS-86` and `ISS-111` (the show-why-and-appeal rule is FD§4.4, not FD§5.5), `ISS-102` (FD§8.3 has no approval mechanism) and `ISS-91` (the ban on execution is FD§10 item 4). None of the underlying findings was dropped.
- **Owner decisions.** `ISS-81`, `ISS-82`, `ISS-85`, `ISS-102`, `ISS-105` and `ISS-113` are `needs_owner` and belong in S3.2 with their defaults. `ISS-82` and `ISS-102` both turn on how many independent people hold reviewer and admin roles in Phase 1 and are best decided together. Only the tail sections of each part were read, as the cell requires.
