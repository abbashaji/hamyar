# 19. Risks, what remains unverified, and a pre-build checklist

*Spec part 19 · extends the Founding Document (FD) risk register FD§8.2 and verification checklist FD§8.3 · uses the vocabulary of `spec/00-conventions.md` (`S0.*`) exactly. Nothing here is legal advice, and nothing here has been verified against any provider's terms or any law. Every legal or provider-dependent statement is tagged [V] for a human reviewer or for counsel.*

## 19.1 Scope, scales and ID schemes

This part does four things. (1) It carries the 15 risks of FD§8.2 forward under stable ids and adds spec-level risks: technical, legal [V], safety, operational and project (19.2, 19.3). (2) It names the tripwires that turn a risk into a concrete action (19.4). (3) It lists everything that remains unverified [V] and says where each answer is stored in the system: a `ServiceRegistryEntry` field, a `LaunchGateItem`, a configuration parameter or a counsel memo (19.5 to 19.7). (4) It gives the pre-build checklist in three separate stages, before coding starts, before the first member and before public exposure (19.8). The open decisions of `S3.*` are referenced, not restated (19.9). [D]

**Qualitative scales** (no probabilities, no invented numbers) [D]

| Scale | Labels | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Likelihood | `low`, `medium`, `high`, `unknown` | A judgment of the spec author [A]. `unknown` means nothing supports a judgment yet; it is never read as `low` and sorts as `high` |
| Impact | `minor`, `moderate`, `severe`, `critical` | `critical`: a member could be endangered, the project could be forced to close, or credit integrity is broken. `severe`: a milestone or a gate fails or trust is damaged. `moderate`: rework or a degraded measure. `minor`: cosmetic or easily reversed |
| Priority | sort by impact first, then likelihood | Two risks never get a multiplied score; labels are not numbers |
| Owner | `owner`, `founders`, `operator`, `reviewer`, `counsel`, `builder` | `owner` is the person who commissions the project; `operator` and `reviewer` are the roles of `S0.8.2`; `counsel` is an external adviser and not a system role [V]; `builder` is whoever implements |
| Status tag | `[V]`, `[H]`, `[D]`, `[P]`, `[A]` (`S0.2`) | `[V]` the risk rests on a fact a human must verify; `[H]` on a hypothesis; `[D]` it is mitigated by a binding spec decision; `[P]` a premise from the FD; `[A]` an assumption of this spec |

**ID schemes added by this part** [A] (reported as `ISS-191`): `RISK-nn` (already in `S0.5`; `RISK-01`..`RISK-15` are the FD§8.2 rows, `RISK-16` and up are spec-level), `UNV-nn` (an unverified item, 19.5), `CQ-nn` (a counsel question, 19.7), `CHK-nn` (a pre-build checklist item, 19.8), `OD-nn` (an owner decision, 19.9). Ids are never reused or renumbered.
